The story so far

A small Midtown salon with a thirteen-year reputation

Alchemy has been part of J Street since 2013 — long enough to become one of Sacramento’s most-reviewed salons, and small enough that every guest still gets a one-on-one consultation.

  1. 2013

    Alchemy opens on J Street

    A luxury salon experience opens in Midtown Sacramento — a beautifully designed room built around customer service first, and making people look and feel beautiful above all.

  2. Along the way

    A name built one guest at a time

    Stylists work one-on-one with every guest, learning their lifestyle and what their hair needs — and stay current on the latest designs and styles. Free consultations for new guests became part of how the salon works.

  3. Today

    Still independent, still on J Street

    4.7 stars from 409 Birdeye reviews, 4.8 on ThreeBestRated, and a #11 ranking among Sacramento’s 206 salons. Booking runs through the salon’s Vagaro page, and current deals live on Instagram at @Alchemysalonandspa.
